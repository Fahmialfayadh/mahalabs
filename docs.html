The Philosophy

MahaLabs is designed to be a living organism. It doesn't rely on complex frameworks or heavy databases. Everything is transparent—files, data, and logic. It allows for rapid experimentation without the overhead of "enterprise" patterns.

The goal is to move from Idea to Implementation in the shortest path possible.
